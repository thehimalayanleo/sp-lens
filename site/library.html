Method

Setup

Each prompt needs a middle step the model never writes out, such as a country it must think of before naming the capital. For each source layer we score every word in the vocabulary with four lenses and keep the top 10.

SP-Lens  =  1.0 · R  +  0.25 · J  +  0.25 · logit  +  0.5 · prev

R is the R-lens, J is the Jacobian lens (mean of dT[t']/dS[t] over later positions), logit is the logit lens, prev is the same readout at the previous position. A word list is cleaned by dropping prompt words and near-duplicates.

Keywords on the cards are a display step that never sees the answer: word-like tokens only, words that show up for 5% or more of prompts dropped, and score = Σ over layers of (1/rank) × log(N/df).

"Found" means one of the hidden step's tokens is in the top 10 at any source layer. This is simpler than the benchmark's pass@10.

Results

Limitations

  • 421 confirmation prompts per model, 6 categories, three small open models.
  • The logit lens is close on Ouro. On Ouro-1.4B order-of-operations it wins (98% vs 95%).
  • Poetry and loose association are near 0% for every lens.
  • The keyword step can drop common correct words, such as "world".
  • These are reading results, not causal ones. A word in the list does not show the model uses it.
SP-Lens, unpublished results. Data: SmolLM3-3B, Ouro-1.4B, Ouro-2.6B on the R/J-lens v2 confirmation set.
